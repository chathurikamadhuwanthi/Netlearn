Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you start learning.

Do I need any prior networking knowledge?

No. The course starts from the very basics and every module builds on the one before it.

Do I need routers or switches to practice?

No. The built-in simulators let you practice subnetting, routing decisions and basic router commands right in your browser.

Is the certificate an official qualification?

No. It is a course-completion certificate issued by the NetLearn platform. Every certificate has a unique ID that anyone can verify on this site, but it is not an accredited qualification.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your completed lessons and quiz results are saved to your account, so you can continue any time.

Can I learn on my phone?

Yes. The site adapts to phones, tablets and computers, though the lab simulators are easiest to use on a larger screen.
